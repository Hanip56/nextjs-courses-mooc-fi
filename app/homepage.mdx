# Welcome to Blog App

Keep track of interesting web development articles and share your favorite blogs.

## What can you do here?

- **Explore blogs** and search by title.
- **Create a blog** when you're signed in.
- **Like articles** you enjoy.
- **Save articles** to your personal reading list.
- **Mark articles as read** when you finish them.

## Get started

Visit the [blogs page](/blogs) to discover articles, or [create an account](/register) to build your own reading list.

This application was built as part of the University of Helsinki **Full Stack Open — Next.js** exercises.
